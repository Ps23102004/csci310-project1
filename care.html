    Garment Fiber Stewardship

    500 GSM French Terry

    Turn inside out before washing. Machine wash cold (max 30°C / 85°F) with gentle detergent. Always lay flat on a drying rack. Never tumble dry to preserve natural cotton loop integrity.

    14.5oz Okayama Selvedge Denim

    Wear raw for at least 4-6 months before washing. When required, submerge in a cold tub bath with wool wash for 45 minutes. Hang dry outdoors in shade away from direct UV sunlight.
